# Moon-Checkers
Enter "Lunar Myth Draughts" a thematic version of Checkers that enters the Plutarchian mythos of the Scribe God defeating the Moon God in a classical rendition of everyone's favourite board game!!! Now you can play as either Thoth or Hermes as you free Rhea or Nut from the curse of Helios against Khonsu or Selene. Coming to Steam if given support.
